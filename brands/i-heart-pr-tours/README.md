# I Heart PR Tours Brand System

**Status:** Gate 1 approved, Gate 2 pending, public publication disabled

**Ownership:** Independent third-party customer identity

**Service credit:** None

I Heart PR Tours invites guests to experience Puerto Rico through thoughtfully guided tours on the island the team loves. The brand voice is warm, locally informed, thoughtful, and adventurous.

## Identity

The seven SVG files in `assets/source/` and the supplied files in `assets/source/favicon/` are exact owner-approved source bytes. Use the dark or light vertical and horizontal lockups for their intended surfaces. Use the colored `heart.svg` as the reduced mark and the source for missing modern application-icon sizes. Matching favicon and platform sizes copy their supplied source files exactly. The sand treatments are approved atmospheric compositions.

Do not edit, normalize, trace, simplify, crop, reframe, recolor, or replace the supplied artwork. Do not recreate its lettering from Poppins, Source Sans 3, or Courier Prime. No standalone wordmark exists. Black and white logo files are derived only through the approved shadow-suppressed, knockout-aware mask transform. Application icons and favicons always retain the colored heart.

## Color

- White `#FFFFFF` and soft paper `#F8F6F2` establish the light-first, white-paper presentation.
- Action red `#C5342C` is reserved for calls to action and important next steps.
- Support blue `#1C5B8D` provides the primary accessible structure, with sky `#68A9DD` and pale sky `#A1CFF4` as supporting fills.
- Brown `#5D4A46` is an occasional warm secondary note.
- White is suitable on deep blue and red for ordinary text.
- Fixed identity colors remain unchanged. When a pairing fails WCAG 2.1 AA, change the role or surface rather than the source color.

## Expressions and atmosphere

`vertical_sand.svg` and `horizontal_sand.svg` are optional atmospheric treatments for selected campaign and editorial contexts. They appear in a dedicated guide section and never replace the core lockups.

## Typography

Poppins Bold 700 is the fixed display face. Source Sans 3 Regular 400 and Semibold 600 are the fixed body faces. Courier Prime Regular 400 is the fixed mono face. The approved OFL binaries live in the repository shared font store so generation remains offline.

## Publication

The generated guide and kit are private Gate 2 review material. Showcase cards, registry entries, hosted guides, releases, deployments, and service credit remain disabled unless the owner explicitly approves a later change.

## Kit entry points

- `brand.json` is the machine-readable source contract and approval ledger.
- `assets/source/` contains exact approved source artwork.
- `identity-continuity.json` binds the canonical source, renderer, and proof matrix.
- Generated kits, PDFs, rasters, and review packets are produced under `dist/` and are never committed.
