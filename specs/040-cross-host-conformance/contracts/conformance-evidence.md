# Contract: Conformance Evidence

## Required profiles

The contract defines phone portrait touch, phone landscape touch, narrow desktop, normal desktop, reduced motion, forced colors or high contrast where the renderer supports it, and enlarged text. Every host track dispositions every profile as supported, unsupported with a renderer reason, pending proof, blocked, or failed.

## Evidence boundaries

| Evidence class | May prove | Must not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `browser-reference` | Generated Web/React specimen in the public browser reference | Tauri, Wails, egui, actual host, or consumer adoption |
| `tauri-reference-host` | Android WebView inset ownership and canonical trace behavior in the Tauri fixture | Actual Glitchpad adoption or hardware certification |
| `wails-reference-host` | Windows window-control avoidance and input/resize behavior in the Wails fixture | Actual go-schedule adoption |
| `egui-reference-renderer` | Native egui rendered-state and unsupported-capability behavior | Actual ESO Weave adoption |
| `actual-host` | Separately supplied host or device result | Consumer product adoption unless that record also exists |
| `consumer-adoption` | Separately authorized consumer revision and pinned kit | Other consumers or later versions |

Verification rejects any result whose evidence class differs from its declared fixture entry point. A missing native track remains pending or blocked and cannot be promoted by copying browser evidence.

## Result identity

Every result records production brand and version, all applicable canon, recipe, adapter, compiler, and conformance versions, source revision, host, renderer, target, exact tool version, profile, status, and diagnostics. Missing or conflicting identity fails before the result can be staged or published.

## Diagnostics

Failures use exactly one primary class: `visual`, `accessibility`, `interaction`, or `host-boundary`. A diagnostic includes a stable code, brand, profile, host, subject, and actionable message. An unknown or generic class is invalid.

## Publication boundary

Generated fixture results may be staged with a verified kit and displayed by the public site. They remain reference evidence. Actual host and consumer adoption records require separately authorized work and cannot be generated by S040.
