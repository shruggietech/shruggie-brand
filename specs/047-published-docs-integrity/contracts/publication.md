# Contract: Published Guidance and Navigation

## Reader-facing prose

The governed manual inventory, hosted guideline instructions, and instructional files shipped in every production kit are public prose. They state current behavior, commands, compatibility, safety boundaries, and verification in direct language. Work-slice identifiers and obsolete project history belong in specifications, changelogs, issues, and provenance records, not operating guidance. A documented exception may preserve an active compatibility term or an actual instruction to invoke Spec Kit, never a vague blanket exemption.

## Portable integration cards

A visual asset card presents the original bytes on a well where meaningful artwork is distinguishable. The well and its labels have explicit foreground/background roles. Nonvisual XML, metadata, declaration, and container deliveries are labeled as resources without implying that an image preview is missing. The same generated portable file is delivered in the kit and hosted by the site.

## Bottom pagination

Previous and next cards remain links with the governed destination URL and order. A fragment-free cross-page activation lands at the destination beginning and moves focus into its main content. An explicit fragment retains its target. Direct navigation and history travel keep their normal semantics. Reduced motion does not animate a reset.

## Failure policy

An audit violation, contrast failure, incorrect scroll/focus result, missing production kit, verifier problem, glyph failure, or publication mismatch blocks the candidate. The checks do not grant waivers for approved identity or WCAG 2.1 AA.
