# Brand Authority Requirements Checklist

This custom checklist tests the quality of the written requirements. It does not certify implementation or record a creative approval.

- [ ] Does the spec identify which observations are user requirements, current website facts, ZIP concepts, and legacy context?
- [ ] Does every proposed production logo source have a defined approval route, exact source identity, and drift rule?
- [ ] Are Full and Reduced roles and small-size evidence defined before a source is called canonical?
- [ ] Are formal identity colors separated from interface cues and required contrast pairings?
- [ ] Is typography approval distinct from observing a font on the existing website?
- [ ] Are social share words, line breaks, and composition governed separately from a tagline or logo lockup?
- [ ] Does publication cover every required website surface with independent client ownership language?
- [ ] Does the spec say what happens when live-site claims drift before publication?
- [ ] Does the required kit inventory come from the repository rather than the ZIP?
