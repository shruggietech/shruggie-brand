# Research: S057

## Existing generator and site path

`gen_logo.py` builds a 1280 by 640 `social-preview` from `brand_idea` and records it as a lockup. This conflates editorial brand idea and approved slogan, hides the image among logo lockups, and omits I Heart PR Tours because that brand has no standalone wordmark. `prepare_site.py` independently draws a generic route card for every metadata image; brand landing pages therefore do not use the kit's identity image.

## Identity continuity

Go-schedule's reduced master has two exact SVG path records, a blue prompt and mint cursor. Its Full master has thirteen older elements. The generator uses Full for standard marks, paired lockups and larger icons. Copying the exact reduced records into Full promotes the intended geometry without redrawing. `identity-continuity.json` hashes both roles and requires current topology and source rationale. Its historical constructed state remains truthful.
