# Documentation Pagination Contract

- Each available neighbor is one anchor containing direction, destination title, and optional description.
- First pages expose only next; middle pages expose previous and next; last pages expose only previous.
- Rest, hover, focus, and active fills use the theme's neutral card surface.
- The title uses foreground at semibold weight; the description uses readable muted foreground at regular weight; both remain smaller than ordinary article text.
- A persistent neutral border strengthens to the accessible green interaction accent on hover and focus.
- Cards have no CTA wash, inset line, lift, native underline, or decorative underline.
- Direction and destination remain visible before interaction, targets are at least 44 pixels high, and labels wrap at 360 pixels and 200 percent zoom.
