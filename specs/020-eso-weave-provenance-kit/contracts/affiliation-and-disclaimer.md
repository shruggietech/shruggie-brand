## Prohibited output

ESO Weave output must not state or imply:

- `A ShruggieTech project`;
- ShruggieTech ownership, parentage, endorsement, maintenance, official publication, support, or warranty;
- official affiliation with, endorsement by, or support from ZeniMax Online Studios, ZeniMax Media Inc., Bethesda Softworks, or Microsoft;
- that automation is authorized by game terms or safe from account action.

Missing or contradictory affiliation values fail before any publishable output. The generated-output scan covers human documentation, PDF source, HTML, manifests, registries, framework bindings, metadata, and public projections.

## Required vendor boundary

Standalone and public contexts that could imply official status state that ESO Weave is unaffiliated with and unsupported by the named companies, that Elder Scrolls marks belong to ZeniMax Media Inc., and that users remain responsible for applicable terms and consequences. Compact metadata may reference the full disclaimer by stable URL only when the visible surrounding surface already identifies ESO Weave as third-party.

## Semantic independence

Independent inheritance requires explicit ESO Weave emphasis and action colors. No generator may fill these roles from ShruggieTech house orange or from a compatibility default.
