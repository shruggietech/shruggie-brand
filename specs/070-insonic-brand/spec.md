# Feature Specification: insonic Brand and Official Showcase

**Feature Branch**: `codex/070-insonic-brand`

**Created**: 2026-10-03

**Status**: Both creative gates approved, final verification and official publication in progress

**Input**: The owner requests a new ShruggieTech brand for `insonic`, with domain `insonic.io`, constructed from the ground up using the latest BrandBuilder. Intended scope: ingest, store, normalize, transcribe, and organize speaker-tagged voice assertions into a knowledge graph while improving speaker recognition and replication with the collection. The owner authorizes Spec Kit construction, interactive questions, and official company brand-subdomain inclusion upon completion.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Choose and approve the identity (Priority: P1)

The owner reviews a product-grounded brief and visual directions, selects a direction, and approves its actual production Full and Reduced masters with exact source and measured proofs.

**Why this priority**: Every later artifact needs an explicitly approved identity.

**Independent Test**: Inspect the selected direction, source inventory, four sizes on four surfaces for both masters, and exact owner decision.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the product description and source documentation, **When** the brief is prepared, **Then** it covers preservation, transcription, speaker identity, assertions, graphs, recognition, and replication while distinguishing current and planned capabilities.
2. **Given** exploratory directions, **When** the owner selects one, **Then** selection authorizes candidate construction without constituting production-source approval.
3. **Given** a qualified candidate, **When** Gate 1 is approved, **Then** the decision binds exact masters, construction method, palette, framing, renderer, and 32 proofs.

### User Story 2 - Approve and receive brand fundamentals (Priority: P2)

The owner reviews lockups, typography, formal colors, interface cues, a representative application, and a distinct social image, then receives the verified kit.

**Why this priority**: Consistent, readable applications and independently approved copy make the identity usable.

**Independent Test**: Review the Gate 2 packet and confirm the final kit has zero mandatory verification problems, zero glyph failures, and inspected visual evidence.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** Gate 1 approval, **When** fundamentals are assembled, **Then** derivatives preserve approved geometry and formal colors are distinct from functional cues.
2. **Given** candidate messaging, **When** wording and uses are approved, **Then** only approved roles publish that text; unresolved candidates remain private.
3. **Given** Gate 2 approval, **When** the kit is compiled, **Then** mandatory checks pass and optional capability skips name their missing tools.

### User Story 3 - Find the brand on the official site (Priority: P3)

A visitor finds insonic in the ShruggieTech brand catalog, inspects approved guidelines, and downloads the matching kit and assets.

**Why this priority**: The owner explicitly requests official publication upon completion.

**Independent Test**: Check catalog, guidelines, registry, social metadata, downloads, and release checksums after validation and publication.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an approved verified kit, **When** it enters the official catalog, **Then** the site projects the same identity, message roles, assets, and version.
2. **Given** the public entry, **When** visitors follow downloads, **Then** links resolve and kit checksums match the release inventory.
3. **Given** private research and review files, **When** the site builds, **Then** those files and personal audio-project data stay outside the public projection.

### Edge Cases

- General green lights and direction selection do not substitute for exact creative approvals.
- Production-source, palette, framing, renderer, or proof drift returns to Gate 1; reviewed derivative or social-copy drift returns to Gate 2.
- Tiny marks retain recognizable silhouettes and intentional separation at 16 and 32 pixels.
- Unknown speakers, quoted positions, and similarity scores do not become unsupported identity, truth, or accuracy claims.
- Public specimens use illustrative data rather than personal media, transcript contents, credentials, or voice vectors.
- Failing accessible values change rather than receiving waivers.
- Owning insonic.io does not require product-site deployment in this brand slice.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The brief MUST separate facts, constraints, proposals, and unresolved choices and cover the complete intended product scope.
- **FR-002**: The identity MUST use `insonic`, `insonic.io`, ShruggieTech ownership and parentage, and the owner-authorized official showcase relationship.
- **FR-003**: The owner MUST receive concrete directions and actual Full/Reduced masters with all 32 proofs before Gate 1 approval.
- **FR-004**: Approved geometry and provenance MUST survive promotion and generation unchanged; governed drift MUST invalidate approval.
- **FR-005**: Gate 2 MUST present wide/stacked lockups, applicable wordmark, formal palette, separate interface cues, typography, representative application, distinct social image, and derivative evidence.
- **FR-006**: Slogan, descriptions, optional strategy messages, social copy, and uses MUST retain independent approved, absent, or unresolved states; no role may silently supply another.
- **FR-007**: Text and meaningful cues MUST meet WCAG 2.1 AA on declared dark/light surfaces and communicate state beyond color alone.
- **FR-008**: Final compilation MUST follow both approvals and deliver identity assets, local fonts, tokens, supported bindings, guides, and zero mandatory verification problems.
- **FR-009**: Official catalog, guidelines, registry, metadata, and downloads MUST consume the verified kit and exclude unapproved candidates.
- **FR-010**: Public material MUST exclude private review and personal project data; source and downloadable text MUST be valid UTF-8 without BOM, LF, and free of mojibake.
- **FR-011**: Existing identities MUST preserve geometry and pass aggregate validation; generated output MUST stay outside version control.
- **FR-012**: Publication MUST follow reviewed change and release workflows, with definitive CI and live route/checksum evidence before official-site completion is claimed.

### Key Entities

- **Working brief**: Discovery topics, independently decided message roles, evidence, and open choices.
- **Identity candidate**: Full/Reduced masters, helper, framing, palette qualification, renderer, and proofs.
- **Approval record**: Owner wording, source, date, scope, candidate identity, and bound digests.
- **Fundamentals packet**: Derivatives, palettes, typography, application, social composition, and manifest.
- **Verified kit**: Versioned generated assets/bindings with measured results and exact inventory.
- **Public projection**: Official catalog, brand routes, approved copy/assets, and matching downloads.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: Both approvals bind actual packets, with all 32 logo proofs and no inferred approval.
- **SC-002**: The kit reports zero mandatory verification problems and zero glyph failures; produced visual evidence is inspected.
- **SC-003**: Every public message has an independently approved role/use and no specimen includes personal source-project data.
- **SC-004**: Declared official brand routes/downloads resolve to the approved version and downloadable checksums match the release inventory.
- **SC-005**: Existing kits pass aggregate validation and no generated kit, PDF, raster export, registry, or archive is committed.

## Assumptions

- The owner has approved lowercase identity, independent Glacial blue, shared house typography/layout, calm precise character, exact production geometry, message roles/uses, applications and both complete creative packets.
- Official company brand-subdomain publication is authorized upon completion, with the approved `A ShruggieTech project` endorsement and no separate service credit.
- Developers building voice and knowledge systems are the approved primary audience. Creative approvals bind the actual packets and source digests recorded below.
- Recordings and facet are read-only product evidence. Processing-engine implementation, audio migration, source-project changes, and actual app integration are outside this brand slice.
- Initial specimens cover brand web use and an audio-library application. Supported platform assets follow the existing kit contract.
