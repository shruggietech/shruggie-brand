## Review conclusion

The producer checks are strong for registry, icon, provenance, adapters, conformance, and archive membership. The demonstrated gaps are the portable root manifest's empty-list success and the marker-only exported-tree audit. S060 closes those without replacing truthful optional states. The matrix is a family inventory; every specific generated file is covered by its family's manifest or publication-tree closure check. The actual full guideline reader-task quality work remains #270.
