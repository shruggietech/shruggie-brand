# Messaging Projection Contract

The public schema declares messaging as independent named roles. Status is approved, absent, or unresolved. Approved records require text, uses, source, approved_by, and approved_on. Other states cannot publish text. Uses are visual-guide, strategy-reference, social-image, site-metadata, and consumer-data. One use cannot authorize another.

PDF, portable, and hosted visual guides display only records allowing visual-guide and preserve text and role labels. Social output continues to use approved social_copy and must agree when a general slogan also allows social-image. Consumer data may expose state and provenance, while display adapters omit unapproved text.

Legacy guide.idea, guide.descriptor, and other brand-specific overrides cannot supersede messaging. A guide visual uses a shipped kit path with matching manifest identity. Empty roles omit their sections without a generated brand claim.
