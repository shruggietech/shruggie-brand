## Independent roles and uses

Only approved text for an explicitly listed use can render on that surface. Keep social composition approval separate because changing it invalidates an approved image.

Alternative: map existing social slogans to reusable slogans. Rejected because #296 forbids cross-filling roles without owner evidence.
