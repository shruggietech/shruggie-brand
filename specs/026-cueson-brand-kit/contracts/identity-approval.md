# Identity Approval Contract

## Gate 1 entry

Gate 1 may begin only when the evidence snapshot, identity criteria, four complete concept records, recommended color system, measurement results, and proof manifest all exist under the recorded S026 revision. Proposal output remains ignored and ineligible for production discovery.

## Gate 1 packet

The operator receives:

- four concept names and stable revision identifiers;
- one recommendation with a product-specific rationale;
- exact vector proposal hashes and proof-manifest hash;
- 256, 64, 32, and 16 pixel light, dark, and single-ink proofs;
- silhouette, clear-space, alignment, contrast, and color-vision measurements;
- represented and missed identity criteria;
- resemblance, cliché, accessibility, and implementation risks;
- the recommended palette's complete semantic role map and relationship to parent-brand roles;
- a direct invitation to approve, reject, or request bounded changes.

## Gate 1 decision semantics

- `approve` applies only to the exact named glyph and palette revisions and hashes shown.
- `improve`, `revise`, questions, silence, or approval of only one part keeps the gate pending.
- Requested improvements create a new revision and a new manifest. Earlier evidence remains historical and cannot authorize production.
- Production `brands/cueson/brand.json`, canonical geometry, canonical palette, derivatives, and public output remain prohibited until both glyph and palette are explicitly approved.
- The approval evidence records exact operator wording, operator identity, date and time, scope, displayed files, hashes, and any rejected alternatives.

## Gate 2 entry

Gate 2 may begin only after current Gate 1 approval, construction of the complete current derivative set, successful focused validation, and generation of an exact derivative manifest and comparison packet.

## Gate 2 packet

The operator receives:

- approved Gate 1 source identifiers and hashes;
- primary horizontal, stacked, mark-only, wordmark-only, slogan, and description compositions;
- full-color, reversed, light, dark, black, white, and single-ink variants;
- full and reduced masters with transition threshold and minimum-size evidence;
- web, favicon, Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, social, repository, and compatibility previews;
- platform crop, mask, clear-space, alignment, and dimension measurements;
- the exact preferred two-line description treatment and permitted one-line alternative;
- vector, outlined-type, and representative raster proofs;
- every derivative's source relationship and hash;
- the exact proposed public surface set and consumer-handoff boundary;
- a direct invitation to approve, reject, or request bounded changes.

## Gate 2 decision semantics

- Gate 1 approval does not authorize Gate 2 typography, spacing, reduced geometry, platform crops, or applications.
- `approve` applies only to the exact derivative manifest and public surfaces shown.
- Requested improvements create a new manifest and keep all public surfaces disabled.
- The approval evidence records exact operator wording, operator identity, date and time, scope, displayed files, source hashes, derivative manifest hash, and public surface set.

## Staleness

Any drift in a bound product-evidence claim, master geometry, palette value, font source, wordmark outline, derivative-producing metric, approval packet, derivative manifest, or public surface definition invalidates the affected approval. S026 returns to the earliest affected gate and does not infer renewed permission.
